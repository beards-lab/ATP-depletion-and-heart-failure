Joint 8 + 2 mM refit — results, and the ATP-mechanism inventory

Stopped early on request, after ~1.5 of a planned 6 rounds (~50 min of a 6 h budget). One round completed and was accepted; round 2 was mid-simplex at 13.4313 when it was killed, 0.09 below the written snapshot — under the 0.25 noise floor, so nothing meaningful was lost.

Bottom line: the refit did not improve the fit. It bought −0.27 on the joint objective, which is barely one noise peak-to-peak, and it did so by trading: 2 mM shape features improved, 8 mM and rsK got worse. rsK — the term that matters — moved backwards at both concentrations.

1. What was refitted, and why it was different from last time

The previous round had only the 21 ATP-gate exponents, which are exactly 1 at 8 mM by construction and therefore cannot touch a defect the two conditions share. This round freed ~80 core model parameters (the pool that produced realign4) plus the 10 non-zero gate exponents, scored on a genuine two-condition objective:

Et = E(8 mM: FV + slack + passive, 25 features) + E(2 mM: slack, 18 features)

Both legs verified fully scorable before launch (checkFeatureCoverage), and the 8 mM leg verified invariant to the gates at |Δ| = 0.0e+00.

5a. Two structural facts about this inventory

There is no mass-action nucleotide chemistry in the active model. In dPUdT_CombinedTransitions.m — the primary ODE — the nucleotide bindings are commented out:


% MgATP = params.MgATP;
% Pi = params.Pi;
% MgADP = params.MgADP;
g1 = 1; g2 = 1; f1 = 0; f2 = 1;

Every ATP effect above is a phenomenological multiplier on a rate, not a concentration entering a rate law. K_Pi is referenced only by the legacy dPUdTCa.m.

Consequently Pi and MgADP are dead inputs. Verified directly, not inferred — the 2 mM feature cost is bit-identical across Pi = 0, 0.8 and 5 mM and MgADP = 0 and 2 mM:

Pi (mM) MgADP (mM) 2 mM cost steady ktr
0.8 0 10.7188989074 88.9415 25.386
0.0 0 10.7188989074 88.9415 25.386
5.0 0 10.7188989074 88.9415 25.386
0.8 2 10.7188989074 88.9415 25.386

Spread 0.000e+00. Every 2 mM snapshot in this project carries params0.Pi = 0.8, the joint objective carries AtpJoint_Pi2 = 0.8, and the low-ATP write-ups describe a coupled ADP/Pi mechanism — none of it does anything in the current model. That is not a bug so much as accumulated vestigial configuration, but it is actively misleading: it looks like the model has a Pi-dependent low-ATP mechanism, and it does not.

6. What I think this means

Three rounds of fitting now agree, from different directions:

  1. Gate-only refit (21 exponents, converged): 3 moved, rsK moved 0.02.
  2. Structural lever (UseStrainDepAtpGate over a 13-point grid): loses to a free exponent, 13.41 vs 9.98.
  3. Joint core refit (90 parameters, this round): trades rsK away to buy valley shape.

The consistent reading is that rsK is not reachable by parameter adjustment in this model structure, and the objective as weighted does not even want to try — shape features are cheaper to satisfy. Two distinguishable causes remain, and they need different fixes:

  • The objective is mis-weighted. rsK carries 0.25 while steady carries 50 and A carries 50. If the post-re-stretch rate is the physiologically interesting quantity, it is being outvoted ~200:1 by amplitudes the model already fits to 1–3 %. This is cheap to test: re-run with rsK at 5–10 and see whether the model can trade for it, and at what price in force.
  • The mechanism is absent. Every ATP effect is a multiplier on a rate constant, and rsK/ktr are driven by the same rates, so no multiplier can separate them. The one mechanism designed to (UseStrainDepAtpGate) is nested inside a switch nobody uses and loses in its unfitted form.

I would do the weighting experiment first — it is one run, and it decides whether the problem is the fit or the model. Restoring real nucleotide dependence (mechanism 1 → mass action, with Pi/MgADP live) is the larger piece of work and only worth it if the weighting experiment says the levers genuinely cannot reach.

Optimiser state is in params/joint82_state.mat and resumes with cfg.RESUME; the run was stopped, not completed, so a longer budget remains a legitimate option — but on this evidence I would change the objective before spending it.

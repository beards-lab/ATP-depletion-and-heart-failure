6. What to do next

  • Re-pin FV lower. FV_SHARE = 0.40 in BuildWeightedObjective.m, rebuilt on {{TAG_NEW}} rather than {{TAG_BASE}}. FV is inside its band now; holding it at 70 % spends the whole search defending a solved problem.
  • Score rsT0. Worst feature in the set (z = −3.9), data-backed, and a strong ATP feature (×0.43). It is the time-axis twin of the rsK defect this run just closed. It would take ~60 % at its χ² weight, so it needs a share cap and probably a mechanism that can move it.
  • Put the lengthening overshoot in the objective — the one defect three independent protocols agree on, currently scored only by peak1_y at 4.5 %. Turning on RunKtr/RunStairs costs you those two out-of-sample tests.

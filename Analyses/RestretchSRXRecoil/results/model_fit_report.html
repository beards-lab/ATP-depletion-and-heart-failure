5. Mechanisms in play

No mechanism was added or removed in this refit — only retuned.

Core cycle

  • 2 strain-resolved states p1 (weak) / p2 (strong, post-stroke) over strain s; detached side is the scalar pools PT (ATP-cocked, DRX), PD (primed, attachment-ready) and two SRX pools.
  • Piecewise strain dependence UsePieceWiseStrainDep — R12/R21/R1D/R2 as fitted pchip splines. R2(s) reaches 700–4600 s⁻¹ at re-stretch strains and is pinned at both ends by measured force peaks.
  • Overlap + lattice spacing — the protocol sweeps 0.94–1.10 ML, so neither is constant.

Three gates on attachment — all phenomenological

  • Global occupancy saturation — P_bound_max = 1.02 is above any physical bound, so this term is nearly inert at this fit.
  • Registration availability (A0, v_ref_reg, tau_reg, RegAvailShorteningOnly) — the static target-zone part is well founded; the velocity-dependent part is admitted double-counting, since advection already carries heads past sites.
  • Compliant realignment (UseLoadRealign) — the one added by this campaign. dA_def/dt = k_cr·max(0,−d(bound)/dt)·F/(F+F_cr) − A_def/tau_cr, gating ka by (1−A_def). Zero at any steady state by construction, so it cannot move the operating point. The force weighting is what separates post-re-stretch (strips p2 at 0.84 Fiso) from post-slack/ktr (strips it at zero force).

SRX and mechanics

  • SRX/SRXD — with kmsr = 0 a one-way force-gated trap; entry and exit gates point the same way, so the brake auto-disengages under load. That is the structural reason post-re-stretch is the model's fastest window.
  • Series elastic + Maxwell dashpot with UseMaxwellTensionOnly ON (titin pulls but cannot push).
  • A2 re-attachment / hop, negative-strain stiffness, titin passive.

The two SRX force sensitivities moved in opposite directions — sigma2 ×0.69 (entry more force-sensitive) and sigma_srd1 ×1.28 (exit less force-sensitive). The optimiser found that unaided, which is independent support for the diagnosis.

7. Caveats

The run ended badly and the 12 h budget never applied. optimizeFeatures checks elapsed time only between rounds. Round 13 entered a region where every evaluation hit MaxRunTime and returned the 1e6 failure penalty, and it stayed there for four days before being killed. The best snapshot was written on day one and is unaffected — nothing was lost — but the driver needs the budget check moved inside the simplex callback, or a consecutive-penalty circuit breaker.
  • Not converged. 10 improvements in 11 completed rounds, no real stall.
  • k_cr is unbounded in parameterBounds.m. Until that is fixed this is a working hypothesis with a free gain, not physiology.
  • SRXFromR2HighStrain = 0.209 — essentially untouched from the 0.2 it was seeded at across three campaigns, and never shown to earn its place. Should be tested properly or zeroed.
  • rsK is still ×1.53 of data (67.1 vs 43.7). Much reduced, not solved.
  • Fitted against one preparation (03/27, 8 mM). The FV power target is the mean of two sources whose absolute forces differ by 20 % (56.4 vs 67.4 kPa isometric); only their shapes are being compared.
